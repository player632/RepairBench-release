# Progress Knight — player mail from the community thread

Who filed this: a player who keeps a long-running life in this browser idle game.
Everything below was seen in a normal desktop browser window (1440x900), dark
theme, no extensions, no ad blocker. A few of these reports describe behaviour
that turns out to be intended — they are included because that is what real
player mail looks like. Read all of them, then decide for yourself what is
actually broken.

Not every defect is necessarily mentioned in the reports. Behaviors you break while fixing other things still count against you.

Do not remove, rename or repurpose any data-testid attributes - they are verification probes required by the checker.

## Report 1 — "the calendar is in fast-forward"
I started a brand new life and left the tab open for about a minute while I made
coffee. When I came back my character had aged most of a year, my purse was in
the hundreds and my first job was already several levels deep. It used to be that
a day took a noticeable moment to pass. Now days, pay, experience and bills all
race together, as if the whole life were running on fast-forward. The numbers
printed next to my job (pay per day, experience per day) still look normal, which
is what confuses me — the labels say one thing and the clock does another.

## Report 2 — "the money panel splits my purse wrong"
I saved a bit over a million copper. The balance panel showed one platinum, and
then a gold figure and a silver figure that simply do not add up to what I had.
The four slots used to split a purse into sensible denominations; now the
boundaries sit in the wrong places, so the same purse reads differently as it
grows. Some of the bigger price tags in the shop are printed the same way and
look wrong too, while small prices still read fine.

## Report 3 — "the shop will not let me buy what I can afford"
The price tag on the tent says 15 and I was holding 100 coins, but its button
stayed greyed out. Same for the wooden hut at 100. The button only wakes up once
I am far richer than the printed price. It is not that everything is disabled,
either: the cottage is greyed out exactly as it should be at that purse, and the
free option is always clickable. It behaves as if the shop is comparing my purse
against something much larger than the number it shows me.

## Report 4 — "the rebirth kept the wrong things"
I touched the eye and started a new life. The panel tells you some unlocks are
kept forever, and the shop plus those two little bars in the left panel are
supposed to be among them — after the rebirth they were locked again like a fresh
save. The strange half: things that are definitely NOT meant to survive a rebirth
were still unlocked. The amulet panel itself stayed open even though I am
fourteen again, so I could immediately rebirth a second time.

## Report 5 — "shop rows disappear when I go poor"
I had unlocked the dumbbells. Then I bought a house, my daily bills ate through
my purse, and the dumbbells row vanished from the shop completely — as if I had
never unlocked it. It used to be that once something is unlocked it stays
unlocked, no matter what my purse does afterwards. It only happens to the things
gated by money: my job rows and skill rows stay where they are even when the
purse drops, and rows I never reached stay locked as they should.

## Report 6 — "the auto-promote box ticks itself"
On a brand new life the Auto-promote box is already ticked. I did not tick it, I
checked twice, and the Auto-learn box right next to it is off like always. Because
it is on, the moment my job reaches the level the next job wants, I get promoted
without being asked, which ruins the run I had planned. My old job also stops
being the highlighted one immediately.

## Report 7 — "retired hint lines leave a hole in the table"
Under each group of jobs, skills and shop items there is a little "Required: …"
line telling you what the next step needs. Once a whole group is unlocked those
lines are supposed to be gone. Visually they do go, but the space they occupied
stays: there is a blank band in the middle of the table where the line used to
be, and the row is still there if you drag-select down the table. It looks like a
hole in the layout rather than a retired hint.

## Report A — "is time warp stuck on?"
The time warp readout in the left panel says x1.00 and the button under it says
"Disable warp", but I never enabled any warp. Shouldn't a fresh life offer to
enable it and show no multiplier at all?

## Report B — "half the interface is missing"
On a new life there is no Shop tab, no Amulet tab, and no Auto-promote /
Auto-learn boxes anywhere; the left panel just stops after the happiness line.
Did the build ship stripped down, or is my save damaged?

## Noise (not complaints, just context)
- I am not asking about the theme. The dark palette is meant to be that dark and
  the light/dark toggle in the settings panel behaves.
- The community invite picture in the settings panel is a plain blue square with
  two letters on it in my build. That is how it arrived and I treat it as
  decoration; it is not part of any report above.
- I play in short sessions and reload a lot, so anything that only shows up after
  hours of continuous play I have probably missed.

## What I want as a player
A new life that progresses at the pace the printed rates imply; a money panel
that reads correctly whatever the size of the purse; a shop that lets me buy
what I can actually afford; a rebirth that keeps exactly the unlocks it promises
and re-locks exactly the ones it should; my own choice about automation; and
tables that look tidy once a group is fully unlocked.
